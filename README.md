# DIGITAL-MARKETING
Digital Marketing is the practice of promoting products, services, or brands using online platforms and digital technologies. It includes strategies such as Search Engine Optimization (SEO), Social Media Marketing (SMM), Content Marketing, Email Marketing, Pay-Per-Click (PPC) advertising, and Analytics to reach and engage target audiences effectively.

The main goal of digital marketing is to increase brand visibility, attract potential customers, generate leads, and drive conversions through data-driven and cost-effective methods. By analyzing user behavior and campaign performance, businesses can optimize their strategies in real time and achieve better results compared to traditional marketing.

Digital marketing plays a crucial role in today’s business environment by enabling organizations to connect with global audiences, build strong online presence, and maintain long-term customer relationships in a competitive digital world.
